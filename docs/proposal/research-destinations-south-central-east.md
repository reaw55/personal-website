# Research: Lesser-Known Destinations in Southern, Central, Eastern and Western Thailand

_Desk research for the trip-planning platform proposal (follow-on to "Designing a Digital Trip Planning Platform to Spotlight Thailand's Lesser-Known Travel Destinations and Foster Local Businesses", NIC-NIDA 2023). Compiled 2026-11-05 under a short time limit. Treat all prices as indicative. Anything marked **approx.** was not confirmed by a source in this session._

## Summary

- **Policy anchor:** In 2018 the Thai government named 55 "secondary" provinces (เมืองรอง) for tourism tax incentives. One definition is provinces with fewer than about 4 million visitors a year. The **South** group has 9 provinces: Nakhon Si Thammarat, Phatthalung, Trang, Satun, Chumphon, Ranong, Narathiwat, Yala and Pattani. **Ratchaburi** is in the 12-province **Central & East** group. Later rounds of the personal income tax deduction were 15,000 THB in 2024, and the government announced a 1.5x multiplier for secondary cities (up to 30,000 THB). Check the current 2026 terms with the Revenue Department.
- **Best fits for a partner network:** Trang, Satun, Nakhon Si Thammarat (Khiriwong), Chanthaburi and Samut Songkhram (Amphawa). Each already has organised community-based tourism (CBT) groups, homestays or seasonal farm businesses with published prices.
- **Price signals found:**
  - Homestays cost about 500–1,200 THB a night.
  - CBT boat or island programmes cost about 800–1,500 THB per person.
  - Shared firefly boats cost about 60 THB per person, and private boats about 600–1,500 THB.
  - Orchard fruit buffets cost about 199–400 THB per person.
- **Gaps:** Bangkok-to-destination times and fares, and Western/Central data (Lopburi, Suphan Buri, Ratchaburi Suan Phueng, Phetchaburi), were not verified this session and come from general knowledge (marked approx.). Prices in USD from OTA listings are converted only loosely.

## Notes per destination

1. **Trang:** The islands have strong eco and "slow travel" appeal, and dugong and seagrass conservation offers a sustainability story. The only baht prices found were on OTAs. Plan to source homestay and longtail rates directly from operators at the Libong and Mook piers.
2. **Satun:** UNESCO Global Geopark status (17 Apr 2018) covers Thung Wa, La-ngu, Manang and part of Mueang Satun. The geopark is designed around community-based, educational tourism, which suits a CBT marketplace well. Sources put it about 980 km from Bangkok.
3. **Chumphon:** This is a secondary province on the official South list. It is the cheapest overland southern option and has a quiet Gulf coast. Its partner base needs primary research.
4. **Nakhon Si Thammarat (Khiriwong):** This is the clearest CBT model here, with an organised homestay group since 1996 and Thailand Tourism Awards. It suits a pilot partner.
5. **Chanthaburi:** The appeal is strongly seasonal (Apr–Jul), so the platform could sell timed "fruit season" packages. Buffet prices in sources are dated (2015–2018), so re-check them.
6. **Trat:** Ban Nam Chiao CBT is known from general knowledge but was not verified in this session's searches.
7. **Ratchaburi:** This province is confirmed on the Central & East secondary list. It is close to Bangkok and suits weekend trips, so families are a good segment.
8. **Samut Songkhram (Amphawa):** This area is mature and partly mainstream, but it still has small homestays and boat operators. Prices vary widely by source, so standard pricing could be a platform feature.
9. **Lopburi and 10. Suphan Buri:** These are cheap, easy day or overnight trips. Data came from general knowledge, so verify them against TAT before use.
